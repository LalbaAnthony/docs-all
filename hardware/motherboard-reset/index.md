# Modern Motherboard BIOS Reset

Reset BIOS:
- Check if your motherboard has a BIOS reset button or jumper.
- If it has a button:
  - Turn off the computer and remove the power cable.
  - Press and hold it for 10-15 seconds while the computer is off.
- If it has a jumper:
  - Turn off the computer and remove the power cable.
  - Press the power button for 10-15 seconds to discharge any remaining power.
  - Use a screwdriver to short the two pins of the clear CMOS jumper on the motherboard for a 10-15 seconds. This will reset the BIOS settings to default.
